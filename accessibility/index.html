Accessibility

Last updated: [launch date]

We want chrisphillips.co to be usable by everyone and aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. If any part of the site is hard to use, let us know through the contact form and we'll help you get the information you need and work on a fix.

What we've done

  • Every page and form can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Form fields have labels, and error messages are announced to screen readers and explain how to fix the problem.
  • Text and buttons are sized and colored for readable contrast, and pages still work when text is enlarged.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the site's animations are turned off.
  • Address suggestions are optional: you can always type your address, or let your browser fill it in.

Known limitations

  • Address suggestions come from Google Maps, which we don't control. Typing the address yourself always works.
  • The Home Seller's Playbook PDF has searchable text but may not read smoothly with every screen reader. If you need its content in another format, ask and we'll send it.

Contact

To report a problem or ask for information in another format, use the contact form. We aim to respond within one business day.
