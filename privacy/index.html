Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Chris Phillips Real Estate ("we," "us") operates chrisphillips.co. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone number, property address, details about your home, timeframe, and any message you send through our forms. Some forms save your answers as you move through each step, so information you enter may be received even if you don't finish the form.
  • Information collected automatically: the page you visited, the referring website, advertising campaign details (such as UTM tags and ad click IDs), browser type, and general usage data collected through analytics tools and cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide real estate services, including preparing market analyses.
  • To contact you by phone, text, and email as described in our form consent.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To comply with legal and brokerage recordkeeping obligations.

How we share it

  • With The James Embry Team and Keller Williams Indianapolis Metro North as needed to provide real estate services.
  • With service providers that run our website and communications (for example, Google for form storage, email, maps/address lookup, and analytics; GitHub for website hosting; Dog CEO for images on our error page). They may use information only to provide their services.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Mobile phone numbers and text message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

We and our analytics and advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to understand site usage and measure ads. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts, use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us through the website to ask us to access, correct, or delete your information.

Data retention

We keep inquiry records as long as needed for the purposes above and for brokerage recordkeeping requirements.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date shows the latest version.

Contact

Use the contact form on this site.
