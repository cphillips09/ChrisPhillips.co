Why not just use an online home value estimate?

Online estimators are a fine starting point, but they only see public records. They can't see your new kitchen, your finished basement, the roof you just replaced, or the busy road behind the house. In Indianapolis neighborhoods where homes vary a lot street to street, that's often the difference between a quick sale and months of price cuts.

Your analysis is done by a person, not an algorithm. I look at the homes buyers will compare yours to right now, including the listings that didn't sell and why, then price your home to hold up with buyers and appraisers alike.

Home value questions

Is the home value analysis really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to list with me. If the numbers tell you to wait, that's a fine answer too.

Is this the same as an appraisal?

No. An appraisal is done by a licensed appraiser, usually for a lender. A home value analysis is a listing agent's opinion of price, based on what's selling, what's competing and what isn't selling near you. It's what you need to decide whether and when to sell, and at what price.

How long does it take?

The form takes about two minutes. I'll reach out within one business day to ask a few questions about your home, then walk you through your price range and estimated net proceeds.

Do you need to see my home?

Not to get started. A quick call about updates and condition is usually enough for a solid range. If you're seriously thinking about selling, a walkthrough sharpens the numbers and lets me tell you what's worth doing before you list.

What if I'm not ready to sell yet?

That's common. Many people request a home value analysis months or a year before they move, to plan the timing and budget for their next home. Just pick "Just curious what it's worth" on the form.
