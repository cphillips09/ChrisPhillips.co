The loop I run every time.

Operations is designing how work gets done so the business hits its goal, whether that goal is speed, cost or flawless service. Five steps, every time.

  1. Identify

    Start with the work you repeat most. In real estate, that's listings and buyers. Define the primary goal, then bring in everyone who does the work or feels its effects. Their input makes the system better and the rollout easier.

  2. Build

    A system is people, tools and process. Write the process first as a checklist, then pick tools that fit the people using them. Admins love spreadsheets. Salespeople don't, so I design around that.

  3. Implement

    Put it to work and watch for friction and ambiguity. A good system removes decisions. Train on the ‘How’ and the ‘Why’, because people execute better when they understand the purpose.

  4. Delegate

    To scale, train others to run the system and take ownership. You keep your eyes on it through reporting feedback. That frees up time for you to build the next one.

  5. Repeat

    Find where the most time or money goes, or what frustrates you every week. If nothing jumps out, it's time to grow: sales, marketing, recruiting. The job is never done.
